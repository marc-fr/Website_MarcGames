About the game

The player controls battleships, inspired from the early World-War II, before that the aviation becomes pre-dominant over the seas.
The goal is the aim and shoot ennemi ships, with realistic and challenging aiming control.

Release history

Release alpha 4 (August 2026):

I worked to improve the UI: the controls are displayed more efficiently. The game shows the predicted trajectory of the shells. This allowed to track and fix some aiming bugs. I reworked the weather system, but still work in progress.

On previous versions, I used volumetric-rendering for some clouds. But that seems excessive in terms of performance. Then, I replaced it by billboards the time being. For the next version, I'll continue to improve the game-play and work on the wheater-system, as well as the ocean rendering.

Release alpha 3 (April 2026):

I improved the player experience, by adding a window that shows the shells hit, and by introducing sound effects. Besides, I continued to work on the rendering and the ocean simulation.

Release alpha 1 and 2 (2025):

Still focusing on rendering, with shadows, SSOA, smokes ...
The scope of the game is now better defined, even if the player experience is still very rough.

Protoptye (July 2024):

With the game being ported to Desktop, I want realistic visuals. Therefore, I worked during a long time on the procedural sky rendering and the ocean rendering.

Protoptye (2010's):

Made with python, the game concept is inspired by the RTS game gendra. The player controls the ships movements and the raw shooting parameters (heading and distance). The gameplay is very dynamic and chaotic.
